A Tapestry of Heritage,
Born on Wooden Pit-Looms.

Founded on the uncompromising refusal of industrial homogenization, कल्रा KRUTI by Vyara stands as a guardian of sacred Indian handloom traditions and heirloom handcrafted sarees.

Each saree is hand-knotted by generational artisans in Varanasi, Kanchipuram, and Chanderi. Using raw, unadulterated mulberry silks, tested 24K electroplated gold zaris, and organic botanical dyeing, our drapes take up to 45 days to materialize.
